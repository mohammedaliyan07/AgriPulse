## 1. Project description

Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and agricultural cooperatives aggregate smallholder produce to
secure fair market pricing. However, farmers under cash pressure frequently break off agreements
("side-selling") to exploitative middlemen, causing cooperative contract default and market supply
failure. **AgriPulse** turns the FPO's own transaction ledger into an early-warning system:

* **Class 2 — Ingestion & sanitation:** multi-branch CSV ingestion with ₹/`Rs.`/`INR` parsing
  (Indian comma groups), quintal/kg/MT → metric-ton conversion, and `DD/MM` vs `MM/DD` date-format
  inference by per-branch majority vote, plus a full QA ledger.
* **Class 3 — Ag-RFM & churn model:** an agricultural adaptation of Recency / Frequency / Monetary
  (days since last harvest deposit, seasons with deliveries, ₹ value routed through the
  cooperative) enriched with yield-retention dynamics and **district rainfall deviation** — the
  context that separates *drought stress* (rain failure, member returns) from *commercial
  side-selling* (intentional defection).
* **Capacity-aware deployment:** FPO extension officers can visit at most **50 farms/month**, so the
  operating threshold τ is tuned for **precision under the visit cap**, and every dispatch list
  carries a drought-stress overlay that routes flagged members to *emergency liquidity + insurance*
  instead of a loyalty intervention.
* **Class 4 — Productization:** a single-file **Streamlit operational dashboard** (`app.py`)
  generated from a structured IBM Bob master prompt, with village risk map, seasonal trends, risk
  register and a downloadable top-50 dispatch list.

### Headline results (out-of-time test, label seasons 2024-K & 2024-R)

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Simulated FPO | 1,690 registered farmers · 48 villages · 8 districts (Maharashtra & Madhya Pradesh) · 12 seasons (2019-K → 2024-R) |
| Clean ledger | 32,208 transactions · ~₹86.7 crore lifetime procurement · 20,162 metric tons |
| Modelling panel | ~5,900 farmer-season rows · next-cycle no-supply (churn) rate ≈ 11.0% |
| Random Forest (final) | ROC-AUC **0.926** · PR-AUC **0.663** · Brier **0.037** (isotonic-calibrated) |
| Capacity-tuned threshold τ = 0.82 | Precision **81.7%** · Recall **52.7%** · precision@50 ≈ **0.68** |
| Diagnostic (<40% retention rule) | **58.6%** raw → **80.3%** in normal-rainfall seasons (validating the ~80% operational heuristic) → **13.6%** in rainfall-deficit seasons |
| Rainfall-feature ablation | removing agro-climatic features costs AUC and multiplies false alarms on drought-stressed members |
| Live 2025-K cycle | 539 members scored · 27 above τ · 131 in drought-stress context · top-50 monthly dispatch list (12 drought-flagged) · ~₹30.1 lakh annual value at risk |

**Ethics guardrail (enforced throughout):** the model flags *association, not causation* — a high
risk score is never evidence of bad faith. Drought-stressed members are routed to relief, not
reprimand; every list is human-reviewed (extension officers decide); farmers are pseudonymous IDs
(DPDP Act 2023-aligned data minimisation); fairness is audited across landholding classes.

## 2. Dataset

The transaction ledger is **synthetic, fully disclosed and reproducible** (`SEED = 42`): the
notebook's §2.1 generates it cell-by-cell, so *no real farmer's data is used or needed*. The
generator was designed against the conventions of real open data so the pipeline transfers without
redesign:

* **AGMARKNET** (AGMRS mandi price/arrival records, Govt. of India) — <https://agmarknet.gov.in>
* **India Meteorological Department** district rainfall normals — <https://imdpune.gov.in>
* FPO structure and behaviour: NABARD's FPO status reports.

> The dataset "link" is therefore the notebook itself: run it top-to-bottom and `data/raw/` is
> regenerated byte-identically. The messiness is deliberate — ₹ 1,23,456.50 Indian-notation amounts,
> quintal/kg/MT mixes, per-branch date conventions (including one branch that mixes MM/DD and
> DD/MM), duplicated rows, invalid dates and missing fields.

## 8. Methodology in one page

1. **Simulate** a 12-season, 8-district FPO ledger with documented behavioural dynamics (loyalty,
   distress shocks, droughts, side-selling) — §2.1.
2. **Sanitise** the messy multi-branch CSVs: ₹ parsing, unit standardisation to metric tons,
   day-first inference per branch, crop/type normalisation, de-duplication, QA ledger — §2.2–2.3.
3. **Diagnose** the "<40% of surveyed yield" retention rule and stratify it by rainfall context — §2.4.
4. **Engineer Ag-RFM features** per farmer-season (recency, frequency, monetary, retention trend,
   input-purchase micro-transactions, rainfall deviation) — §3.
5. **Model** next-cycle no-supply with calibrated Random Forest; validate out-of-time; tune τ
   under the 50-visits/month cap; ablate the rainfall features; audit fairness — §4.
6. **Score the live membership** for the 2025-K cycle and build the drought-aware top-50 dispatch
   list — §5.
7. **Productize** as a single-file Streamlit dashboard via an IBM Bob master prompt — §5 (Class 4).

## 9. Limitations

* The ledger is synthetic (disclosed, seeded) — real FPO exports add unstructured noise; the
  sanitation rules were designed against real AGMARKNET/IMD conventions to transfer directly.
* The "no effective supply" label (<10% of surveyed yield) conflates rare true exits
  (migration/illness) with defection.
* 12 seasons is short for deep models; survival analysis (time-to-defection) is the natural next step.
* Fairness is audited on landholding and drought exposure; extend to more groups when lawfully
  collectable.
